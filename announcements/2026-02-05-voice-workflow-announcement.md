# 🚀 Voice-Enabled Workflow Builder is Live!

**TL;DR:** We've launched a revolutionary voice-driven interface for StreamlineAI, allowing users to build and manage complex automation workflows entirely through natural speech on desktop and mobile.

## What's New
- **Conversational Workflow Creation**: Simply speak your automation needs (e.g., "Connect NetSuite to Slack for large POs") and our AI builds the logic for you.
- **Mobile Voice Builder**: Field workers can now create, modify, and troubleshoot workflows hands-free while on-site.
- **AI-Powered Debugging**: Encounter an error? Just ask "Why did this workflow fail?" and get natural language explanations and fixes.
- **Native Real-time Audio**: Powered by OpenAI's gpt-realtime, offering <500ms latency for a truly natural conversation experience.

## Why This Matters
One of our biggest hurdles at StreamlineAI has been onboarding complexity—it typically takes 45 days for a new customer to see value. The Voice Builder destroys this barrier. By removing the technical friction of a visual drag-and-drop interface, we're enabling non-technical users and field workers to automate their work in minutes, not weeks. 

This directly supports our 2026 strategic priority of reducing time-to-value and expanding platform usage to the 70% of enterprise employees who are mobile-first.

## By The Numbers
- **Time-to-Value**: Reduced from 45 days to **14 days**.
